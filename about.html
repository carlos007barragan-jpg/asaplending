Operators first. Lenders because investors needed one.

ASAP Lending was founded in Kansas City by Carlos Barragan, an investor and builder who buys, renovates and builds homes in the metro. The company finances the kind of projects its own principals take on.

How we lend

Structure. Transparency. Execution.

01

Structure

First-position loans on investment property, documented clearly and closed at a title company with title insurance.

02

Transparency

You see your costs in writing before you commit. We tell you who we are and what our role is in your loan.

03

Execution

We do not promise approvals before underwriting, and we do what the term sheet says once it is issued.
