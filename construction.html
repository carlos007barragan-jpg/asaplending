Questions

Construction loan questions

How is the budget released?

In draws. The budget is split into phases at closing. When a phase is done, an inspection verifies it and that phase is paid.

How quickly are draws paid?

Request the draw when the phase is complete. We schedule the inspection right away and release once it is verified, so the next phase can start.

Do you finance the lot?

Send the lot details with the build. We structure land and construction together when the project supports it.

What counts as heavy rehab?

Projects that go past cosmetics: structural work, additions, full mechanical replacement or a gut renovation.

Do I need building experience?

We need to see who is building it. An experienced licensed builder or general contractor on the project matters as much as your own résumé.
