Questions

Construction loan questions

How is the budget released?

In stages. Money is released as completed work is verified. The schedule for your project is written into your terms.

Do you fund ground-up construction in every state?

New construction depends on the borrower and the state. Send an application with the property and borrower information and we will tell you where it stands.

Do you finance the lot?

We are open to looking at it. Include the lot in your application and we will tell you what we can do.

What counts as heavy rehab?

Projects that go past cosmetics: structural work, additions, full mechanical replacement or a gut renovation.

Do I need building experience?

It depends on the borrower and the project. Tell us about your experience and who is building, and we will tell you where the deal stands.
