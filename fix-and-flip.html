Questions

Fix and flip loan questions

How much can I borrow?

Loan size comes from three numbers: purchase price, rehab budget and ARV. We size it on your deal and put it in writing before you commit to anything.

How do rehab draws work?

Your rehab budget is set aside at closing. Finish a phase, a quick inspection verifies it, and that phase's money is released. You never front the whole rehab.

Can I close in my LLC?

Yes. We lend to LLCs and other business entities.

What if I decide to keep the house?

Finish the rehab on the flip loan, then refinance into a DSCR rental loan and hold it.

What happens if the rehab runs long?

Tell us early. Your written terms spell out the loan term and what your options are if you need more time.
