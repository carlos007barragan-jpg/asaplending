Questions

What investors ask us first

What kinds of financing do you offer?

Fix and flip loans that cover purchase and rehab, DSCR loans for rentals, construction and heavy-rehab loans funded in stages (new builds depend on borrower and state), and transactional funding for double closings. If your deal does not fit one of those, send it anyway and we will tell you what we can structure.

Why don't you post rates?

Because a posted rate is rarely the rate you get. Each loan is priced on its own numbers: the property, the scope of work, your experience and your exit. Send the deal and you get written terms for that deal.

Do you check credit or income?

The deal carries the weight. Our asset-based loans have no credit requirement, and we do not ask for W-2s or tax returns on them. Some other loan types do need a credit check. Applying never pulls your credit: we walk you through the programs first, and credit is only pulled if the program needs it or you choose one that does, before your term sheet is written.

I have never done a flip. Will you look at my deal?

Yes. We fund first-time investors when the deal's underwriting is sound.

How fast can you close?

Days on a clean deal, not weeks. Timing depends on title work and valuation, and we tell you the realistic date up front.

Where do you lend?

Missouri, Kansas, Florida, Texas, Arkansas and Nebraska. Our office is in Kansas City.

Do you lend on a home I plan to live in?

No. We finance investment property for business purposes only.
