The one ratio

Rent divided by payment

DSCR stands for debt service coverage ratio. Divide the monthly rent by the full monthly payment. At or above 1.0 the property pays for itself, and that is what we lend on.

Questions

DSCR loan questions

What DSCR do I need?

At 1.0 the rent covers the full payment. Stronger coverage earns better terms. We look at each property on its own numbers.

Do you verify my personal income?

No. The property's rent qualifies the loan, which is why DSCR works for self-employed and full-time investors.

Can my LLC be the borrower?

Yes. We lend directly to your LLC.

Is this for purchases or refinances?

Both. Buy a rental, or refinance a flip you finished and want to keep.

The house is vacant. How do you count rent?

With market-rent support for the property in place of a lease.
